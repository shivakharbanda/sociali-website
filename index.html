SUCCESS STORIES

CASE STUDY 01

AMILI Bio+Me

CLIENT CONTEXT

AMILI and Bio+Me sought to create a recognizable brand that communicated the science of gut health in a simple, human way.

CHALLENGE

To make complex microbiome insights accessible while differentiating in a fast-growing wellness market.

APPROACH

We conducted a full brand audit, refining their visual identity and narrative. The website was rebuilt with a focus on education and seamless user experience. Social media campaigns extended the brand voice through authentic stories, testimonials, and expert-led webinars.

OUTCOME

The strategy led to a 300% increase in site traffic and 250% growth in followers, alongside a surge of positive customer feedback reflecting stronger trust and engagement.

CASE STUDY 02

Elioe by Watsons

CLIENT CONTEXT

In Malaysia's beauty market, CR8 launched Elioe by Watsons as premium yet accessible skincare, driving in-store traffic and online sales.

CHALLENGE

Spark widespread awareness, cement Elioe as affordable luxury, and boost sales through targeted traffic in a saturated market.

APPROACH

A virtual media briefing brought the story to life, featuring executive insights and product deep dives, securing features in leading beauty and lifestyle outlets that amplified the message.

OUTCOME

The payoff was evident in heightened engagement and a strong growth foundation. Smart PR converted curiosity into loyalty, driving significant in-store traffic.

CASE STUDY 01

Mission Foods #PerfectTogether

CLIENT CONTEXT

Mission Foods sought to elevate brand relevance beyond core cuisine perceptions, positioning as a versatile choice for modern Malaysian households.

CHALLENGE

Elevate brand relevance beyond core cuisine perceptions and create meaningful connections with diverse audiences.

APPROACH

Integrated influencer-led storytelling combined with media events. Launched the #PerfectTogether campaign partnering with local celebrity chefs to create fusion recipes and organized exclusive tastings.

OUTCOME

Achieved over RM2M+ earned media value with award recognition. Won Silver at the Dragons of Malaysia for Best Use of Public Relations.

CASE STUDY 02

MasterChef AI Stars

CLIENT CONTEXT

The iconic cooking show returned with a new AI-integrated format and needed to generate buzz among a younger, tech-savvy demographic.

CHALLENGE

Balancing the traditional love for cooking with controversial new AI elements without alienating core viewers.

APPROACH

Integrated media solution combining traditional billboards with interactive AR social filters. Influencers competed against AI-generated recipes in real-time.

OUTCOME

Record-breaking viewership for the premiere episode. Awarded "Best Media Solution" at the Spark Awards for Media Excellence.

CASE STUDY 01

FinTech SecurePay Rebrand

CLIENT CONTEXT

A legacy payment gateway needed to modernize perception while retaining trust credentials in a competitive fintech landscape.

CHALLENGE

Modernise perception while retaining trust credentials, balancing innovation with the reliability expected from an established financial service.

APPROACH

UI/UX overhaul combined with motion content education. Complete redesign of app and website with "Security made Simple" content pillar utilizing motion graphics to explain complex features.

OUTCOME

40% retention uplift within 3 months plus national press features. The rebrand was featured in major tech publications as a benchmark for financial UI design.

CASE STUDY 02

The Grand Virtual Concierge

CLIENT CONTEXT

A luxury hotel chain faced reduced footfall post-pandemic and needed to reconnect with high-net-worth travelers digitally.

CHALLENGE

Replicating the white-glove service experience in a purely digital environment before the guest even books.

APPROACH

Developed a "Virtual Concierge" campaign using personalized email marketing and an immersive 3D website tour. Targeted LinkedIn and Instagram ads focused on exclusivity.

OUTCOME

Direct bookings increased by 55%, significantly reducing dependency on third-party booking platforms (OTAs).
