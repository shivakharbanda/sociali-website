CLIENT

SUCCESS STORIES

Elioe by Watsons

In Malaysia's beauty market, CR8 launched Elioe by Watsons as premium yet accessible skincare, driving in-store traffic and online sales.

A virtual media briefing brought the story to life, featuring executive insights and product deep dives, securing features in leading beauty and lifestyle outlets amplified the message.

The campaign's objectives were crystal clear: spark widespread awareness, cement Elioe as affordable luxury, and boost sales through targeted traffic. CR8 kicked things off with a press release and media kit that highlighted the brand's unique value—innovative formulas meeting everyday needs.

The payoff was evident in heightened engagement and a strong growth foundation. In an era where skincare is self-care, CR8's approach reminded us that smart PR isn't just about launches—it's about lasting impressions that convert curiosity into loyalty.

AMILI Bio+Me

CLIENT CONTEXT

AMILI and Bio+Me sought to create a recognizable brand that communicated the science of gut health in a simple, human way.

CHALLENGE

To make complex microbiome insights accessible while differentiating in a fast-growing wellness market.

APPROACH

We conducted a full brand audit, refining their visual identity and narrative. The website was rebuilt with a focus on education and seamless user experience. Social media campaigns extended the brand voice through authentic stories, testimonials, and expert-led webinars.

OUTCOME

The strategy led to a 300% increase in site traffic and 250% growth in followers, alongside a surge of positive customer feedback reflecting stronger trust and engagement.
